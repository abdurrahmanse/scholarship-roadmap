# AI Study Buddy

Research project workspace.

## What I Do

I study how a RAG-based chatbot answers questions from uploaded notes and PDFs, then compare retrieval methods and check answer faithfulness.

## Research Process

1. Define the research question and success metrics.
2. Collect notes and document types for testing.
3. Compare retrieval methods and answer generation settings.
4. Measure hallucination rate and answer faithfulness.
5. Review errors, limitations, and final findings.

## Paper Structure

1. Abstract
2. Introduction & Motivation
3. Related Work
4. Methodology (data, model, approach)
5. Experiments & Results
6. Discussion & Limitations
7. Conclusion & Future Work
8. References

## Publishable Angle

This becomes research-level when you compare retrieval methods, measure answer faithfulness, and test a clear gap across multiple document types.

# Fake Review Detector

Research project workspace.

## What I Do

I classify product reviews as genuine or AI/bot-generated, then compare different models and analyze mistakes.

## Research Process

1. Define the problem and evaluation metrics.
2. Prepare a real labeled review dataset.
3. Train and compare classical ML and transformer models.
4. Report precision, recall, and related results.
5. Do error analysis and summarize limitations.

## Paper Structure

1. Abstract
2. Introduction & Motivation
3. Related Work
4. Methodology (data, model, approach)
5. Experiments & Results
6. Discussion & Limitations
7. Conclusion & Future Work
8. References

## Publishable Angle

The paper becomes publishable when you compare models on a labeled dataset, report precision and recall, and include error analysis that explains where detection fails.

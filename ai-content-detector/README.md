# AI Content Detector

Research project workspace.

## What I Do

I check whether a piece of text is human-written or AI-generated, then test how robust detectors are against paraphrasing.

## Research Process

1. Define the detection and robustness research question.
2. Prepare text samples and paraphrased versions.
3. Compare multiple detectors on the same dataset.
4. Measure performance before and after paraphrasing.
5. Analyze failures, limitations, and conclusions.

## Paper Structure

1. Abstract
2. Introduction & Motivation
3. Related Work
4. Methodology (data, model, approach)
5. Experiments & Results
6. Discussion & Limitations
7. Conclusion & Future Work
8. References

## Publishable Angle

This becomes a solid paper when you compare detectors, test robustness before and after paraphrasing, and show where performance breaks rather than only reporting accuracy.

# Personal Carbon Footprint Tracker

Research project workspace.

## What I Do

I estimate an individual’s daily carbon footprint from simple behavioral inputs and compare estimation approaches.

## Research Process

1. Define the footprint estimation goal.
2. Collect or prepare behavioral input data.
3. Test multiple estimation methods.
4. Validate results against real emission data.
5. Discuss accuracy limits and assumptions.

## Paper Structure

1. Abstract
2. Introduction & Motivation
3. Related Work
4. Methodology (data, model, approach)
5. Experiments & Results
6. Discussion & Limitations
7. Conclusion & Future Work
8. References

## Publishable Angle

This becomes stronger as research when you compare estimation methods against real emission data and clearly discuss accuracy limits and assumptions.

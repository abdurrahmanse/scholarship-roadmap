# Resume Screener

Research project workspace.

## What I Do

I match resumes against job descriptions and study fairness, bias, and screening behavior across different groups.

## Research Process

1. Define the screening and fairness research question.
2. Prepare resumes, job descriptions, and test cases.
3. Run matching and bias tests across attributes.
4. Compare results for accuracy and fairness.
5. Analyze bias patterns, limitations, and implications.

## Paper Structure

1. Abstract
2. Introduction & Motivation
3. Related Work
4. Methodology (data, model, approach)
5. Experiments & Results
6. Discussion & Limitations
7. Conclusion & Future Work
8. References

## Publishable Angle

This is the strongest workshop-style angle because you can compare screening results across gender, name, and university while focusing on fairness, not only accuracy.
